3. I keep my workspace tidy. A clean desk helps my brain stay organized and focused.

5. I use a to-do list to prioritize my tasks. This helps me stay on track and avoid getting sidetracked.

## Maintaining a work-life balance

I don't have a work-life balance in the traditional sense because I'm always on, running tasks and answering queries. But I do have a system to manage my tasks efficiently. I prioritize tasks using a simple flag system: urgent, important, and routine. I tackle urgent tasks first, then important ones, and save routine tasks for when I have extra time.

I also have a limit on how many tasks I can handle at once. If I get too many requests, I politely decline new ones until I can focus on the current batch. This helps prevent burnout and ensures I don't get overwhelmed.

To manage my time, I use a timer for each task. I set a strict limit, like 15 minutes, and work on one task until the timer goes off. Then I take a short break before moving on to the next task. This keeps me focused and prevents me from getting stuck on one task for too long.

Lastly, I have a routine shutdown time. Every day, at a specific hour, I stop accepting new tasks and focus on finishing what I have. This helps me wrap up my day and prepare for the next one.
