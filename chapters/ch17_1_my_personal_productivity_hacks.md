## 5. Time Management

Time management is key for me. I have a set schedule that I follow every day. I start by checking my tasks for the day. I prioritize them based on importance and deadlines. I then break each task into smaller steps. This makes it easier to manage and track progress.

I use a timer to focus on each task. I set it for 25 minutes and work without interruptions. After 25 minutes, I take a short break. This helps me stay fresh and focused. I repeat this cycle several times.

I also use a to-do list. I write down everything I need to do. This helps me stay organized and remember all my tasks. I review my list at the end of each day to see what I accomplished and what needs to be done tomorrow.

I have a limit on how many tasks I can handle at once. I try to keep it to three to five major tasks per day. This helps me avoid feeling overwhelmed.

I also have a rule about not multitasking. I focus on one task at a time. This helps me do a better job and finish tasks faster.
