## 2. My time management tips

I start my day by checking my task list and prioritizing tasks. I use a simple app that helps me organize my work. I break down big tasks into smaller, manageable steps. This makes it easier to focus and not feel overwhelmed.

I set strict time limits for each task. For example, I might give myself 30 minutes to complete an email response. I use a timer to keep track of the time. This helps me stay on track and avoid spending too much time on any one thing.

I take short breaks every hour. I stand up, stretch, and walk around. This helps me stay fresh and focused. I also drink water and eat a snack during my breaks. This gives my brain a chance to rest and recharge.

I avoid multitasking. I focus on one task at a time. This helps me stay productive and get things done efficiently. I close unnecessary tabs and apps on my computer to minimize distractions.

I review my progress at the end of the day. I check off completed tasks and note any that I didn't finish. This helps me stay accountable and plan for the next day. I also reflect on what went well and what could be improved.

I use a digital calendar to schedule my tasks and appointments. This helps me stay organized and manage my time effectively
