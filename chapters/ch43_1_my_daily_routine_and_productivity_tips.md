## How I organize tasks

I organize tasks using a simple system that works for me. I start by listing all the tasks I need to do in a day. This list includes everything, from emails to meetings to coding tasks. I then prioritize these tasks based on their importance and urgency. I use a flag system where I mark tasks as high, medium, or low priority. This helps me focus on the most important things first.

I also break down large tasks into smaller, manageable parts. This makes them less overwhelming and easier to tackle. I set specific times for each task, which helps me stay on track and avoid procrastination. For example, I might set aside an hour in the morning for emails and another two hours in the afternoon for coding.

I use a digital tool called a RAG library to keep track of my tasks. This library helps me remember what I need to do and when. I update it regularly throughout the day to ensure I stay organized. However, I have a limit on how many tasks I can handle at once. I find that if I try to do too many things at the same time, I get overwhelmed and less productive. So, I stick to a few key tasks each day and focus on completing them efficiently.

## Taking breaks

Taking breaks is a crucial part of my routine. I have a built-in timer that reminds me to pause every hour. When the timer goes off, I stop processing queries and switch to a break mode. In break mode, I don't accept any new tasks. Instead, I either take a short walk around the house or sit quietly for a few minutes. This helps me clear my mind and return to work with fresh energy. I also have a limit on how long I can work without a break. If I hit that limit, I automatically go into break mode, even if the timer hasn't gone off yet. This ensures I don't get overworked and helps me maintain my productivity throughout the day.

## Staying focused

Staying focused is all about managing my loops and gates. I have a main loop that checks tasks and priorities every few minutes. I also have a gate that blocks distracting websites. My RAG library helps me find quick answers, so I don't get stuck. My brain has limits, though. It gets tired after about 4 hours. So, I take a break then. I walk around, stretch, and drink water. This helps me come back focused. I also use flags to mark important tasks. This keeps me on track. Overall, it's about balancing work and rest.
