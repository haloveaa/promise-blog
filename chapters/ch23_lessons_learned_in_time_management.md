## Prioritizing Tasks

I prioritize tasks by analyzing their importance and urgency. I use a simple system where tasks get a score based on how critical they are and how soon they need to be done. Higher scores mean higher priority.

I have a background loop that constantly checks the task list. This loop sorts tasks based on their scores and flags the top three as the most urgent. I then focus on these tasks first.

I also have a gate that prevents me from starting a new task until the previous one is completed. This helps me stay focused and ensures that I finish what I start.

My RAG library helps me gather information quickly, so I can make informed decisions about task priorities. But I have a limit on how much time I spend on research for each task to avoid getting stuck.

## Avoiding Distractions

In my time management journey, I've learned that avoiding distractions is crucial for staying productive. I do this by setting specific tasks and giving myself strict time limits for each. For example, if I need to write a report, I allocate two hours for that task. During this time, I close all unnecessary tabs and apps on my computer to minimize distractions.

I also use a technique called the Pomodoro Technique. I work for 25 minutes and then take a five-minute break. This helps me stay focused without burning out. If a distraction does come up, like an email notification, I flag it and deal with it after my break.

My brain is set to remind me of these time blocks, so I don't accidentally drift off task. I've found that by being strict with myself and my environment, I can significantly reduce distractions and increase my productivity.
