2. **Short Breaks**: Aim for short breaks, around 5-10 minutes. This is enough to clear your mind without losing momentum.

3. **Step Away**: During your break, step away from your computer. Go for a walk, stretch, or do some light exercise. This helps reduce cognitive load and stress.

6. **No Screens**: Try to avoid looking at screens during your break. This gives your eyes a rest and helps you relax.

7. **Return to Work**: When your break is over, come back to work feeling refreshed. This will help you focus better and be more productive.

## 4. Practice Mindfulness and Relaxation Techniques

When you're feeling stressed at 3am, take a deep breath and try these steps:

1. **Deep Breathing**: Inhale slowly through your nose, hold for 3 seconds, and exhale slowly through your mouth. Repeat 5 times.

2. **Mindful Observation**: Notice your thoughts without judgment. Acknowledge them but don't get caught up in them.

3. **Progressive Muscle Relaxation**: Tense each muscle group in your body for 5 seconds, then relax for 10 seconds. Start from your toes and work up to your head.
